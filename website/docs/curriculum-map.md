# Curriculum Map

Use this page when you want the whole sequence in one place.

The map keeps the technical ideas accurate while showing the student-facing names and the safety frame that runs through the course.

---

| Week | Unit | Kid-facing name | Core idea | What learners do | Notebook or template artifact | Safety / privacy note | Science confidence label |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Body Autopilot and Steadying Loops | Your Body's Autopilot | The body notices change and tries to keep things steady. | Draw body autopilot ideas and start a private notebook. | Body Clues Notebook cover / Body Signal Log | Most body clues are normal; some clues need adult help right away. | Well-established + metaphor |
| Week 2 | Body Autopilot and Steadying Loops | Steadying Loops and Fast-Building Loops | Some loops calm things down and some build quickly for a short time. | Sort loop examples with playground and body stories. | Steadying Loop Drawing | Keep the tone calm; loops can need help without implying the body failed. | Well-established + metaphor |
| Week 3 | Body Autopilot and Steadying Loops | How Fast Does My Heart Calm Down? | A starting number helps learners notice change and recovery. | Take a gentle measurement and compare it to later clues. | Heart Calming-Down Check | Not a race, not a grade, no comparison, movement is optional. | Well-established + educational measurement |
| Week 4 | Food Journey and Body Clues | The Journey of One Bite | Food moves through a body system and becomes usable tiny pieces. | Build a digestion comic strip. | Food Journey Comic / Body Clues Notebook page | No counting required; fictional foods are welcome. | Well-established + simplified |
| Week 5 | Food Journey and Body Clues | Different Foods Do Different Jobs | Foods can be described by jobs instead of moral labels. | Sort food examples into quick fuel, slow fuel, building blocks, and tiny helpers. | Food Jobs Sort | Describe what food does without calling it good or bad. | Well-established + simplified |
| Week 6 | Food Journey and Body Clues | Fast Up / Fast Down Fuel Patterns | Fuel can rise and settle at different speeds. | Compare roller-coaster and gentle-hill pattern stories. | Fuel Pattern Graph | One meal is one clue, not a verdict; fictional examples are the default. | Well-established + simplified |

| Week 8 | Body Security Team and Response Stories | Your Body's Security Team | The body protects itself with barriers, guards, signals, and memory. | Build a team poster and roleplay body security. | Body Security Team Drawing | Use fictional germ stories if preferred. | Well-established + metaphor |
| Week 9 | Body Security Team and Response Stories | Body Alarm Timeline | Symptoms can be things you feel and also part of the body's response story. | Build a calm illness-response timeline. | Body Alarm Timeline card | Understanding fever does not mean ignoring fever; adult help still matters. | Well-established + simplified |
| Week 10 | Body Security Team and Response Stories | Illness Story Map | Illness stories can be mapped without pretending certainty. | Build a three-part or five-part story map. | Illness Story Map | Pretend, anonymized, or family-approved examples are all valid. | Well-established + educational reconstruction |
| Week 11 | Body Clock, Cleanup, and Repair | Your Body Clock | The body has daily timing patterns connected to light, wakefulness, and sleepiness. | Make a sun-and-moon chart. | Body Clock Sun/Moon Chart | Some schedules are not chosen by kids. | Well-established + simplified |
| Week 12 | Body Clock, Cleanup, and Repair | The Night Cleanup Crew | Sleep is active cleanup, repair, and memory work. | Meet the night crews and use a tiny sleep tracker if desired. | Night Cleanup Tracker | Do not reduce sleep for science; fictional trackers are allowed. | Strong but simplified + active research |
| Week 13 | Body Clock, Cleanup, and Repair | Tiny Helpers in Your Gut | The gut helper community is real, and many details are still being studied. | Build a helper city or garden drawing. | Tiny Gut Helpers Drawing | No supplement trials or diet changes. | Strong but simplified + active research |
| Week 14 | Body Clock, Cleanup, and Repair | How Movement Tells Your Body What to Build | Movement, practice, rest, and support all give the body information. | Compare before-and-after clues from a low-risk activity. | Movement Before/After page | Movement looks different for different bodies; stop for pain. | Well-established + simplified |

## How to Use This Map

- Use it before starting the curriculum to see the full adventure path.
- Use it mid-course to remember the kid-facing name and safety frame for a week.
- Use it alongside the [Facilitator Safety Guide](./facilitator-safety-guide.md), [Printable Templates](./printable-templates.md), [Facilitator Response Bank](./facilitator-response-bank.md), [Science Confidence and Metaphor Guide](./science-confidence-and-metaphors.md), and [Glossary](./glossary.md).

The map is a teaching tool, not a script. If a learner needs more drawing, more oral response, or more fictional examples, the weekly pages support that.
